HOW TO PLAY

  1. Each deal arrives with a bad price. Your job: get the Fairness Committee to sign off before the 7:00 AM board call.
  2. Pull levers on the desk papers: WACC, terminal growth, SBC, management cases, EBITDA add-backs, peer and precedent selection, the equity bridge. Every move costs clock time.
  3. The offer must clear the gold committee line on enough valuation bars of the football field, and your Smell Test score has to stay above the bar. Stuff the terminal value too hard and the committee notices.
  4. Run out of time and the MD signs it himself. It's still fair. It's always fair.
Secret level: if the committee kicks you back, refuse to accept it.

A work of satire. All companies, deals and people are made up. Nothing here is advice, financial or moral.
